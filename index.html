How it works

Simple steps: your call or request for a market, we walk your complex or site, then we handle the rest.

  1. The Walk

    On a scheduled visit to your location we look at the possible space, check measurements for fit and proper access to power. We also finalize your employee count and foot traffic, then we match your market and your requested products.

  2. The Install

    The market is delivered and installed professionally in the agreed spot, stocked with snacks and beverages, giving your employees and clients an immediate upgrade.

  3. Smart Restocking

    Every market carries up-to-date technology that reports live sales and system health. We see what's in demand and restock on a smart schedule.

  4. Full-Service

    GlassFront Markets is a one-stop shop. If anything's off, our customer QR codes are on every market front. A quick scan puts the power of communication in your hands: request a product, leave a Google review, or report a problem, in English or Spanish. The QR code sends it directly to me, for same-day follow-up within hours or even minutes. It makes our market service one of a kind.

Is a market a match for your space?

  • Demand is key. Large companies and small workforces have a need that sometimes goes unseen. The right market gives busy employees a place to energize and refresh without leaving the building, and that keeps them safe.
  • 24/7, 8 days a week. Long days, grueling shifts, overnights and weekends: employees need cold drinks to stay hydrated and snacks to keep their energy up on the move. A well-known market with fresh snacks keeps your business running smoothly and morale high.
  • Quick access. Break rooms, hallways, waiting areas, office corners, tight spots. Almost any space can become a refreshment center or a GlassFront Market.

Questions owners ask

What does it cost?

Nothing. The market, the stocking and the service are ours. Your employees pay for what they buy, and the market is an amenity you give them.

What goes in the market?

A first mix based on places like yours, then whatever your employees actually buy. They can scan the QR code on the front and ask for what they want. We adjust on the next restock.

How often is it restocked?

The market reports its sales to us live. We restock before it runs low, and a busy market sees us more often. You never have to call to say it is empty.

How do people pay?

Tap a card or a phone, swipe a card, or use cash. Every market on our route takes all three.

What if something breaks or a purchase fails?

Scan the QR code on the front or call the number on it. It reaches the owner, you get a same-day reply, and we fix our own machines.

We already have a machine from another company.

If it is old, has no card reader, or sits empty, call us. Vending repair is part of what we do. We will look at the space and talk honestly about fixing it or replacing it.
