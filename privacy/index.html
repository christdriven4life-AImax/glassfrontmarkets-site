Privacy

GlassFront Markets is a locally owned vending business in the Denver metro. This page says what we collect, why, and who sees it. Updated October 21, 2026.

When you use a machine's QR code

The request form on each machine's page sends us what you choose to type: what the request is about, the item, any note, and your name or phone only if you want a reply. It goes to our own Google Form. We use it to stock the machine and fix problems, and to reply when you ask us to. The machine number is filled in for you; we do not collect your location.

When you call or email us

We keep your message so we can answer it and serve your location.

Our office tools ("GFM Operations")

GlassFront Markets runs its own office software. With our permission, it signs in to our own Google accounts to read, sort and edit our business email (sales receipts, supplier mail, customer requests), draft and send our replies, and read and update our own calendar, contacts, files and sheets (including the sheet behind the request form). It only ever accesses GlassFront Markets' own accounts. It is not offered to the public, and it never signs in to anyone else's account.

Data it reads stays with GlassFront Markets on our own computers and our own Google accounts. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone else, except where the law requires. Its use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What we never do

We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.
